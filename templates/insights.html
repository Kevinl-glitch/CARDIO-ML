Understanding CVD

What CVD is, how it affects life and how the model helps.

Cardiovascular disease (CVD) is an umbrella term for conditions that affect the heart and blood vessels — including coronary artery disease, heart attack, and stroke. Lifestyle, metabolic health (blood pressure, cholesterol, glucose), and age are common drivers.

  • Impact: Reduces physical capacity, increases long-term care needs, and raises risk of sudden acute coronary events.
  • Prevention: Early detection of risk factors (BP, cholesterol, BMI, glucose, activity) decreases long-term adverse cardiovascular risk.
  • Model role: The ML model identifies complex multi-factor patterns in historical clinical data to estimate risk probabilities — useful for screening and triage, not an autonomous diagnosis.
Predictions are for research/educational use. Always consult healthcare professionals for definitive medical decisions.

Ideal ranges

Common healthy clinical targets

Blood Pressure < 120 / 80 mmHg
Cholesterol (Total) < 200 mg/dL
Fasting Glucose 70 – 99 mg/dL
BMI 18.5 – 24.9 kg/m²
Resting Heart Rate 60 – 100 bpm
Physical Activity ≥ 150 min/week

Note: Values outside these clinical ranges can synergistically increase cardiovascular risk when multiple indicators are elevated concurrently.
